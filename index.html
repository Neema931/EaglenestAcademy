01 Principal's Welcome

A school where
every child thrives.

At Eagle Nest Academy, we believe education is about more than passing exams — it is about shaping confident, curious, compassionate young people ready for the world.

Guided by Kenya's Competency-Based Curriculum, our teachers create learning experiences that ignite creativity, build critical thinking and celebrate each learner's unique strengths. From our Early Years classrooms to our Junior School, every child is known by name, challenged to grow, and encouraged to dream big.

03 Why Eagle Nest

Everything a child needs
to truly flourish.

Six commitments woven into every school day — because great education is never just one thing.

01

CBC-Rooted Teaching

Every lesson is designed around the CBC's seven core competencies — from critical thinking to digital literacy — so learning is always relevant.

02

Small Class Sizes

With a maximum of 25 learners per class, every child receives the individual attention, feedback and encouragement they deserve.

03

Safe, Inclusive Culture

Anti-bullying policies, pastoral care teams and a zero-tolerance approach to exclusion mean every child feels genuinely safe and valued.

04

Modern Facilities

Purpose-built science labs, a well-stocked library, ICT suites and spacious outdoor play areas create the ideal environment for active learning.

05

Vibrant Co-Curricular

From football and swimming to robotics, drama and scouts — 18 clubs and teams help every child discover a talent and build lasting friendships.

06

Strong Parent Partnership

Regular progress reviews, an open-door policy and a dedicated parent portal keep families informed and actively involved in their child's journey.

05 How to Join Us

Four simple steps to
your first day.

Full admissions guide ↗
  1. STEP 01 ✦

    Enquire & Tour

    Reach out via our contact form or call us to book an informal campus tour and meet the team — no obligation, just a friendly conversation.

  2. STEP 02 ✳

    Submit Application

    Complete the online application form and upload your child's previous school reports, birth certificate and passport photos.

  3. STEP 03 ♡

    Assessment Day

    Your child spends a half-day at school so our teachers can understand their learning needs and ensure we set them up for success from day one.

  4. STEP 04 ↗

    Offer & Enrolment

    Receive your formal offer letter, confirm your place with the first-term deposit, and collect your welcome pack. Welcome to Eagle Nest!

Intake periods: January, May and September. Early applications strongly encouraged. View fees & funding ↗
